## The show in one line
A curious boy in a Vietnamese conical hat and his big, always-hungry uncle travel the world
to taste every kind of dumpling, chasing the stories his late father used to tell.

## Characters
- **The Boy:** Small and curious, in a conical hat and a white kimono. Every episode he turns a dumpling into a life lesson ("Life is like a dumpling: what matters is what's inside.").
- **The Uncle:** His dad's brother. Big, loud and hungry. He's the comic relief, always trying to eat the dumpling before the lesson is over.
- **Dad (memory):** Only appears in short flashbacks or a voice-over. He's the reason for the journey.

## Season plan
One dumpling per ~2-minute episode. Countries with several dumplings get **3 episodes: 3 dumplings from 3 different places** (steamed, fried and boiled mixed); every 3rd episode adds a recipe + recap. The route follows Dad's journal along the Silk Road.
Full season map (Silk Road: Season 1 by sea, Season 2 by land, finale in Venice): **`production/series-format.md`**.
Ready scripts: Episodes 1–9 in `scripts/` (China, Korea, Japan). Draft: `episode-21-momo-darjeeling`. Full route: `production/season-map.md`.

## Monetization notes
- Make each episode a real mini-story with an original script. YouTube doesn't monetize mass-produced or repetitive content (its "inauthentic content" policy), so avoid near-identical AI clips.
- Every episode is ~2 minutes (vertical). After each country arc, publish the 3 episodes joined as one 16:9 video (~6 min): it builds watch hours, which Shorts views don't.
- Design note: the Boy wears a Vietnamese conical hat (nón lá) and a white kimono, a look that is clearly his own and doesn't resemble existing famous characters.
