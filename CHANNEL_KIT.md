## The show in one line
A curious boy in a straw hat and his big, always-hungry uncle travel the world
to taste every kind of dumpling, chasing the stories his late father used to tell.

## Characters
- **The Boy:** Small and curious, in a straw hat. Every episode he turns a dumpling into a life lesson ("Life is like a dumpling: what matters is what's inside.").
- **The Uncle:** His dad's brother. Big, loud and hungry. He's the comic relief, always trying to eat the dumpling before the lesson is over.
- **Dad (memory):** Only appears in short flashbacks or a voice-over. He's the reason for the journey.

## Monetization notes
- Make each episode a real mini-story with an original script. YouTube doesn't monetize mass-produced or repetitive content (its "inauthentic content" policy), so avoid near-identical AI clips.
- Shorts lead to subscribers. Once you're making episodes regularly, add a 3–5 minute "compilation" or long episode each month. Long videos earn more from ads per view.
- Design note: the straw hat is generic, but make sure the Boy's look (colors, outfit, face) is clearly your own and doesn't resemble existing famous characters.
