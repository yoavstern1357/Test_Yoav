# Episode 10 (Nepal arc, part 1, draft): "Uncle Climbs a Mountain for Lunch" (Momo, Nepal)

> Draft written before the 3-part arc format. Before production, add the travel transition, the "Legend says…" history beat and the journal clue (see `production/series-format.md`).

**Length:** about 50s · **Location:** a Himalayan mountain trail, then a tiny tea house at the top

## Script

**[0–3s] HOOK.** Wide shot of a huge snowy mountain. A tiny UNCLE at the bottom, panting.
> **UNCLE:** The dumplings are… UP THERE?!

**[3–8s] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #03 MOMO · Himalayas, Nepal`
> **BOY (V.O.):** Dad said the best momos in the world are at the top of the mountain.

**[8–24s] THE GAG.** Fast montage of the climb:
- The BOY hops happily from rock to rock.
- UNCLE is carried by a very unimpressed goat.
- UNCLE sees a "MOMO →" sign, sprints and faceplants into the snow.
- A tiny old woman passes them both, carrying a steamer basket up the mountain with ease.

**[24–34s] THE REWARD.** The tea house. Steam. A plate of momos with a bright red chutney. UNCLE takes a bite of the chutney: fire breath, but a happy one. The goat eats one too.

**[34–44s] DAD'S WISDOM.** The BOY looks out at the clouds below. Sepia DAD silhouette standing at the same view.
> **BOY:** Dad said: *"The hardest climbs lead to the warmest dumplings."*

**[44–50s] BUTTON.**
> **UNCLE:** *(mouth full)* Next time, the dumplings come to *us.*
> *(The goat steals his last momo.)*
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Dad was right… Where to next? 👇
