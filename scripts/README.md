# Episode scripts: The Dumpling Boy

> **v6 UPDATE (2026-11-28).** The format in this README was rewritten for the v6 structure: **60-second story episodes** and **separate 60-second recipe Shorts**, 1 upload per week. The old 2-minute "Arrival / Culture / Recipe + Recap" episodes (scripts `episode-01` to `episode-09` in this folder, `archive/`) are **extended-cut references only**, not the live scripts. Live format: `production/series-format.md` (v6) and `production/v4/series-structure-plan.md` (v6, with the episode table and calendar). Live script base: `episode-01-v4.5.md`; the skill `write-episode-script` produces new scripts. **Every script is shown to the creator in Hebrew in chat and approved before production.**

Every country is a 3-episode **arc** (Arrival, Culture, third episode with the arc **stamp**). After every 2 story episodes a **separate 60-s recipe Short (R1, R2...)** about a new bonus dumpling is published. Ep 1 is split into two episodes: **Episode 1** (soup dumpling + scroll legend) and **Episode 2** (Dad, the book, the hook). Plain consecutive numbering, no "1A/1B": story Ep 1-39; recipe Shorts R1-R18.

## Story episode beat sheet (60 s, about 114 spoken words, vertical 9:16)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-3 s | **Hook:** the funniest or most surprising image. No logo intro. |
| 3-5 s | **Title hit:** `#NN DUMPLING · CITY` card over the 3-s sting of the song |
| 5-11 s | **Host + place:** the Boy to the lens, one map clip |
| 11-22 s | **Learning beat:** the local master shows the secret, 1-2 facts said once |
| 22-40 s | **Gag or BATTLE:** the Uncle's hubris and collapse, or a 15-20 s "Bell Round" cook-off / taste battle (fairness rules in the plan, 3.9) |
| 40-48 s | **Legend scroll** (arrival episodes, 8 s max, "Legend says") or the **serial beat** (rival, or Dad's trace). Exactly one serial beat per episode |
| 48-54 s | **Heart:** Dad's line or the Uncle's quiet line (the arc **stamp** in the 3rd episode of an arc). No jokes near it |
| 54-57 s | **Next hook:** an unresolved question about a person, plus the next place |
| 57-60 s | **"Dad was right..."** and a 1-s camel sting |

Recipe Short template (60 s, standalone, no serial beat): see the plan, 4.2.

## Characters (current canon, see `production/character-bible.md`)
- **Boy ("Bo"):** about **7**, Vietnamese conical hat with an orange chin ribbon, faded indigo tunic, rolled hemp trousers, orange sash, straw sandals. Warm, curious, deadpan. Says the "dumpling wisdom" lines.
- **Uncle ("Uncle Fong"):** Dad's older brother. Big, fully bald, thin curled mustache, earth-brown linen mandarin-collar jacket, wide orange sash, towel on the shoulder. Loud, warm, always hungry, the comedy engine. Jokes are on his behavior only, never his weight. **No secret and no quarrel** (v6).
- **Grandma, Pepper (rival host, from Ep 6), Auntie Clip, the Narrator, the Camel:** see the plan, 3.4-3.5.
- **Dad:** only a sepia silhouette from behind or a calm voice. Dad **vanished** and left his dumpling book; his fate is never stated in Season 1 (one gentle beat at Huế). No jokes within 10 s of a Dad beat.

## Production tips
- One recurring catchphrase for the Uncle before every bite: *"For dumpling research!"*
- End every episode on the same line from the Boy: *"Dad was right..."* It is the show's signature.
- Burn subtitles into the video. Most Shorts are watched without sound.
- Title formula: `{Uncle's problem} vs {dumpling}` (for example "Uncle vs. Soup Dumpling").
- Recipes: GROWN-UP STEP badges, doneness temperatures (71 C / 160 F pork, 74 C / 165 F chicken), an allergy line, a meat-free option.
