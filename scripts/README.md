# Episode scripts: The Dumpling Boy

Format: see **`production/series-format.md`**. Every country is a 3-episode arc:
**1. Arrival** → **2. Culture** → **3. Recipe + Recap**: every episode about **2 minutes**, vertical (9:16).
The table below is the beat for the Arrival episodes:

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook:** a funny or surprising first line or image. No logo intros, because Shorts viewers swipe away. |
| 3–10s | **Arrival:** a new place and a new dumpling, with an on-screen title card (`🥟 #01 XIAOLONGBAO · Shanghai`) |
| 10–35s | **Gag:** the Uncle tries to eat it wrong or too fast, and it goes badly |
| 35–45s | **Dad's wisdom:** the Boy remembers his father and turns the dumpling into a life lesson |
| 45–55s | **Button:** a final laugh, plus "Where to next? Comment!" to drive comments |

## Characters
- **Boy:** About 10, wears a Vietnamese conical hat and a white kimono. Curious, calm, a little poetic. Says the "dumpling wisdom" lines.
- **Uncle:** His late father's older brother. Big, loud, warm and always hungry. The comedy engine.
- **Dad:** Only a soft voice-over or a warm, sepia flashback silhouette. Never fully shown, which keeps it mysterious and emotional.

Optional names: **Bo** (the Boy) and **Uncle Fong**. Change them to anything you like.

## Production tips
- Keep **one recurring catchphrase** for the Uncle before every bite: *"For science!"*
- End every episode on the same line from the Boy: *"Dad was right…"* It becomes the show's signature.
- Burn subtitles into the video. Most Shorts are watched without sound.
- Title formula: `{Uncle's problem} vs {dumpling} 🥟` (e.g. "Uncle vs. Soup Dumpling 🥟").
