## Safety and sensitivity checks
- The burn is steam, a red face and a tiny voice; an ice pack follows and the Uncle is fine. No child copies him. The safe method (lift, spoon, tiny hole, wait, sip) is shown in full in the same episode.
- Legend always hedged ("Legend says" plus the on-screen asterisk "competing versions exist"); competing cities are not named on screen (the tug-of-war is cut).
- No brand, no shop sign text, no jokes on a place, language or people; no use of the "mantou" pun etymology.
- The camel, the floating Uncle, the metro camel and the paper-cut hero with the Uncle's face are fiction. "Dad burned his tongue too" is a story beat.
