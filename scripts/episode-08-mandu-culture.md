# Episode 08: "Uncle Folds 100 Dumplings Alone" (South Korea arc, part 2 of 3: Culture)

**Length:** about 60s Short · **Location:** the warm home of the Han family in Seoul, the evening before Seollal (Lunar New Year), then New Year's morning · **Dumpling:** Mandu (and tteok-mandu-guk)

## Script

**[0–4s] HOOK.** UNCLE sits alone behind a mountain of wrappers, folding at top speed. Flour everywhere. A wall clock spins.
> **UNCLE:** Ninety-eight… ninety-nine… *(collapses)* …help.

**[4–8s] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #08 MANDU FOR LUNAR NEW YEAR`

**[8–22s] CULTURE MOMENT.** Wide shot: the cozy floor of a Korean home. The whole HAN family sits around a low table: HALMEONI HAN (Grandma, tiny, silver bun, big smile), MOM, DAD HAN and two kids, MIN-JI (8) and JUN (5). Everyone has a job: Mom mixes, Grandma folds, the kids press and pinch, Dad lines them up on trays.
> **BOY (V.O.):** Before Seollal, Korean New Year, many families make lots of mandu… together.
> **HALMEONI HAN:** Alone, it takes all night. Together? One song!

**[22–34s] THE GAG.** UNCLE joins the line. Grandma hands him one wrapper. He folds it, passes it to Min-ji, she pinches it, Jun gives it a tiny "boop" with his finger. A perfect mandu! UNCLE stares at his lonely pile of 99 lumpy ones.
> **UNCLE:** *(whispering)* …I did it wrong for an hour.
> **JUN:** *(patting his belly)* It's okay, Uncle!

**[34–44s] NEW YEAR'S MORNING.** Sunlight. Steaming bowls of **tteok-mandu-guk**: white rice-cake slices and mandu in clear soup, with egg strips and green onion on top.
> **BOY (V.O.):** New Year's Day: rice-cake soup, with dumplings!
> **MIN-JI:** An old saying: one bowl makes you one year older!
> **UNCLE:** *(on his third bowl, freezes)* …Then I'm *very* old.

**[44–54s] THE STORY MOVES.** Grandma notices the BOY's conical hat. She smiles slowly and brings out an old family photo: a tall man in the **same hat**, seen from behind, sitting at this very table, folding with the family. (Sepia, no face.)
> **HALMEONI HAN:** He came one New Year. He said: "Make them together."
> **BOY:** *(whispers)* Dad…

The BOY opens the journal: under Seoul, a faded line glows: `Secret #3: make them together.` ✨

**[54–60s] BUTTON.**
> **HALMEONI HAN:** Stay. Tomorrow we cook more.
> **UNCLE:** *(already wearing an apron)* I'll fold. Slowly. With *friends.*
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Dad was right… Next: Grandma Han's family recipe! 👇

## Notes
- **Making mandu together for Seollal:** a well-documented family tradition (often dozens or even hundreds at a time, many frozen for later). It is less common today because of busy lives, so the script says "many families", not "every family".
- **Tteok-mandu-guk:** tteokguk (sliced white rice-cake soup) is the classic New Year's Day dish; adding mandu is very common, and in northern regions dumpling soup (manduguk) is especially traditional. Garnishes like egg strips and green onion are standard.
- **"One bowl = one year older":** a traditional saying linked to the old Korean age system, where everyone turned a year older at New Year. South Korea officially uses international age since 2023, so it is framed as **"an old saying"**.
- **Characters:** the Han family is fictional. Show them proud and warm; all jokes are on the Uncle. Keep on-screen text in English (no invented Korean signs).
- **Dad continuity:** the old photo shows Dad's sepia silhouette only (from behind), never his face.
