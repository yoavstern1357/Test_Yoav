# Episode 02: "The Grandma Who Folds Faster Than Light" (China arc, part 2 of 3: Culture)

**Length:** about 60s Short · **Location:** a busy morning street in Shanghai · **Dumpling:** Xiaolongbao

## Script

**[0–4s] HOOK.** Early morning. A tiny GRANDMA at a dumpling stall folds dumplings so fast her hands are a blur. *Whoosh whoosh whoosh.*
> **UNCLE:** *(jaw drops)* She's… a superhero.

**[4–8s] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #02 HOW SHANGHAI EATS XIAOLONGBAO`

**[8–22s] CULTURE MOMENT.** The BOY and UNCLE sit at a small table on the street. People on bikes, steam from many stalls.
> **BOY (V.O.):** In Shanghai, lots of people eat soup dumplings for breakfast, hot from the steamer, with black vinegar and thin strips of ginger.

GRANDMA holds up one dumpling and counts the folds with a smile. On-screen: tiny folds light up one by one.
> **GRANDMA:** Many folds = a careful cook. Some famous shops aim for 18!

**[22–36s] THE GAG.** UNCLE tries to fold one himself. He makes a lumpy, giant "dumpling" the size of his head. Grandma pats his shoulder kindly.
> **GRANDMA:** Good start. *(gives him a tiny perfect one instead)*
> **UNCLE:** *(eats it slowly, very proud of himself)* Patience!

**[36–50s] THE STORY MOVES.** GRANDMA notices the BOY's conical hat and freezes. She takes an old photo from her apron: a tall man in the **same hat**, smiling at her stall, years ago.
> **GRANDMA:** I know this hat. He came every morning. He said: "the soup is the secret."
> **BOY:** *(whispers)* Dad…

The BOY opens the journal: under Shanghai, a faded line appears: `Secret #1: patience.` ✨

**[50–60s] BUTTON.**
> **GRANDMA:** Come back tomorrow. I'll teach you to make them.
> **UNCLE:** *(already tying an apron)* We're not leaving.
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Next episode: we make soup dumplings at home! 👇
