# Episode 04 (Japan arc, part 1, draft): "Uncle Fights a Frying Pan" (Gyoza, Japan)

> Draft written before the 3-part arc format. Before production, add the travel transition, the "Legend says…" history beat and the journal clue (see `production/series-format.md`).

**Length:** about 45s · **Location:** a lantern-lit Tokyo alley stall

## Script

**[0–3s] HOOK.** Sizzle! UNCLE holds a frying pan lid like a shield, the pan spitting oil.
> **UNCLE:** It's ATTACKING me!

**[3–8s] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #02 GYOZA · Tokyo, Japan`
> **BOY (V.O.):** Dad always said Japan would teach us about balance.

**[8–22s] THE GAG.** The old stall CHEF slides a plate across the counter: gyoza with perfectly crispy golden bottoms. UNCLE wants to make his own. He flips the pan like a pro chef… and the gyoza fly up and stick to the ceiling. One slowly drips back down into his open mouth.
> **UNCLE:** *(chewing, proud)* …Chef's kiss.

**[22–32s] THE LESSON.** The CHEF shows the BOY a gyoza from the side. Captions:
- `Bottom: crispy.`
- `Top: soft.`
- `Inside: juicy.`
> **BOY:** One dumpling. Three textures. Wow.

**[32–42s] DAD'S WISDOM.** Sepia silhouette of DAD at a stove.
> **BOY:** Dad said: *"Be like a gyoza: a little tough on the outside, soft on the top, and full of good stuff inside."*

**[42–48s] BUTTON.** UNCLE looks up. A gyoza is still stuck to the ceiling.
> **UNCLE:** We are NOT leaving that one behind.
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Dad was right… Where to next? 👇
