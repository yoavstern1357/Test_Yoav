# Episode 05: "The Secret Sauce Is… Three Sauces" (Japan arc, part 2 of 3: Culture)

**Length:** about 60s Short · **Location:** Chef Taka's small gyoza counter in a Tokyo side street, lunchtime · **Dumpling:** Gyoza

## Script

**[0–4s] HOOK.** UNCLE sits at the counter with a giant mixing bowl in front of him, about to pour in *every* bottle on the counter at once.
> **UNCLE:** The more sauce, the more science!

**[4–8s] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #05 HOW JAPAN EATS GYOZA`

**[8–24s] CULTURE MOMENT.** Lunchtime. Office workers, students and a grandpa with his granddaughter sit along the counter. Each tray: gyoza, a bowl of rice, miso soup. Further down, someone slurps ramen with gyoza on the side.
> **BOY (V.O.):** In Japan, gyoza is an everyday meal. Lots of people eat it with rice, or next to a bowl of ramen.

CHEF TAKA takes UNCLE's giant bowl away with a smile and gives him a tiny dish instead. He adds three things, and each one lights up on screen: `soy sauce` · `vinegar` · `a few drops of chili oil (rayu)`.
> **CHEF TAKA:** Soy, vinegar, a little chili oil. Mix your own.

**[24–34s] REGIONAL STYLES.** Quick map pop-up of Japan, cartoon postcards flip by:
- `Hamamatsu: gyoza in a circle, bean sprouts in the middle 🌼`
- `Tokyo (Kamata): crispy "wings" ✨`
> **BOY (V.O.):** Some cities have their own style. In Hamamatsu, gyoza come in a circle, with bean sprouts in the middle!

**[34–40s] THE GAG.** UNCLE dips a gyoza in his tiny dish. A drop of chili oil. His face turns bright red. Steam puffs from his ears, *poot poot*. He quietly adds more vinegar.
> **UNCLE:** *(tiny voice)* A *few* drops. Got it.

**[40–52s] THE STORY MOVES.** CHEF TAKA notices the BOY's conical hat. He pauses, then reaches under the counter for an old **guestbook**. He opens it to a yellowed page: a hand-drawn gyoza in the same pen style as Dad's journal, and one line of writing.
> **CHEF TAKA:** A man in this hat drew this. Long ago.
> **BOY:** *(reading, whispers)* "Crispy and soft together." …Dad.

The BOY opens the journal: under Tokyo, a faded line glows into place: `Secret #2: balance.` ✨

**[52–60s] BUTTON.**
> **CHEF TAKA:** Come back tomorrow. We'll cook together.
> **UNCLE:** *(already wearing a white headband just like Chef Taka's)* I was born ready.
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Dad was right… Next: crispy gyoza at home! 👇

## Notes
- The bean-sprout circle is usually credited to one specific Hamamatsu shop. Don't name it; "In Hamamatsu" is enough.
- Mixing your own sauce at the table is common in Japan, and the ratio is personal. Keep the "few drops" of chili oil, since it's a family show and the gag is on the Uncle's impatience, not on the food being "too spicy".
- Chef Taka is a fictional character. His shop has no name on screen and no real signage.
