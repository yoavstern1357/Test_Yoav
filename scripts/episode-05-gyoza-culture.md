# Episode 05: "The Secret Sauce Is… Three Sauces" (Japan arc, part 2 of 3: Culture)

**Length:** about 2:00 Short (1:50–2:10), vertical 9:16 · **Location:** Chef Taka's small gyoza counter in a Tokyo side street, lunchtime · **Dumpling:** Gyoza

## Script

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK.** UNCLE sits at the counter with a giant mixing bowl in front of him, about to pour in *every* bottle on the counter at once.
> **UNCLE:** The more sauce, the more science!

**[0:03–0:06] TITLE CARD.** `🥟 #05 HOW JAPAN EATS GYOZA`

**[0:06–0:16] LUNCHTIME IN TOKYO.** The short cloth curtain over the door flutters. Bicycle bells outside, a distant train tune, the sizzle of the pan. CHEF TAKA calls a warm welcome to every guest who walks in. UNCLE, thinking it's for him, waves back every single time.
> **CHEF TAKA:** Irasshaimase! Welcome!
> **UNCLE:** *(waving, flattered)* Thank you! Thank you!
> **BOY:** He says that to everyone.

**[0:16–0:32] CULTURE MOMENT.** Office workers, students and a grandpa with his granddaughter sit along the counter. Each tray: gyoza, a bowl of rice, miso soup. Further down, someone slurps ramen with gyoza on the side.
> **BOY (V.O.):** In Japan, gyoza is an everyday meal. Lots of people eat it with rice, or next to a bowl of ramen.

CHEF TAKA takes UNCLE's giant bowl away with a smile and gives him a tiny dish instead. He adds three things, and each one lights up on screen: `soy sauce` · `vinegar` · `a few drops of chili oil (rayu)`.
> **CHEF TAKA:** Soy, vinegar, a little chili oil. Mix your own.

**[0:32–0:50] THE GAG (escalating).**
1. UNCLE dips a gyoza in his tiny dish. A drop of chili oil. His face turns bright red. Steam puffs from his ears, *poot poot*. He quietly adds more vinegar.
> **UNCLE:** *(tiny voice)* A *few* drops. Got it.
2. Way too much vinegar. His lips pucker into a tiny fish mouth. He adds soy to fix it.
> **UNCLE:** *(puckered)* Sho… shour.
3. Soy, vinegar, chili, more soy… He finally tastes it, and his eyes go wide with joy. CHEF TAKA leans over, sniffs, and nods.
> **UNCLE:** I invented a new sauce!
> **CHEF TAKA:** *(smiling)* That's my sauce.
> **UNCLE:** *(proud)* …Great minds.

**[0:50–1:06] REGIONAL STYLES.** Quick map pop-up of Japan, cartoon postcards flip by:
- `Hamamatsu: gyoza in a circle, bean sprouts in the middle 🌼`
- `Tokyo (Kamata): crispy "wings" ✨`
- `Utsunomiya: a big gyoza festival every November 🎉`
> **BOY (V.O.):** Some cities have their own style. In Hamamatsu, gyoza come in a circle, with bean sprouts in the middle!
> **BOY (V.O.):** And every November, Utsunomiya throws a giant gyoza festival.

UNCLE, in a dream bubble, rides a gyoza-shaped parade float, waving like a king.
> **UNCLE:** *(dreamy)* Book the tickets.

**[1:06–1:18] HEART MOMENT.** The lunch crowd thins. The BOY is quiet, looking at the empty stool beside him. UNCLE notices. He slides a clean little dish and the three bottles over to the BOY.
> **UNCLE:** *(softly)* Mix one for your dad.

The BOY mixes carefully: soy, vinegar, one single drop of chili oil. He sets it in front of the empty stool. UNCLE nods, and for once doesn't touch it.

**[1:18–1:40] THE STORY MOVES.** CHEF TAKA sees the little dish, then the BOY's conical hat. He pauses, then reaches under the counter for an old **guestbook**. He opens it to a yellowed page: a hand-drawn gyoza in the same pen style as Dad's journal, and one line of writing.
> **CHEF TAKA:** A man in this hat drew this. Long ago.
> **CHEF TAKA:** He mixed his sauce just like that. One drop.
> **BOY:** *(reading, whispers)* "Crispy and soft together." …Dad.

The BOY opens the journal: under Tokyo, a faded line glows into place: `Secret #2: balance.` ✨

**[1:40–1:50] CLIFFHANGER.** The BOY turns the guestbook page. Under the drawing, a second, smaller note in Dad's handwriting: `Taka, keep my pan safe.` The BOY looks up.
> **BOY:** His… pan?
> **CHEF TAKA:** *(a small, knowing smile)* Tomorrow.

**[1:50–2:00] BUTTON.**
> **CHEF TAKA:** Come back tomorrow. We'll cook together.
> **UNCLE:** *(already wearing a white headband just like Chef Taka's)* I was born ready.
> **BOY:** *(to camera)* Dad was right… Next: crispy gyoza at home! 👇

## Notes
- The bean-sprout circle is usually credited to one specific Hamamatsu shop. Don't name it; "In Hamamatsu" is enough.
- Mixing your own sauce at the table is common in Japan, and the ratio is personal. Keep the "few drops" of chili oil, since it's a family show and the gag is on the Uncle's impatience, not on the food being "too spicy". The escalation ends with the Uncle "inventing" Chef Taka's own sauce, so the joke is on him, never on the sauce.
- Festival: say "every November" only, no specific dates or prices on screen (they change each year). The parade float is the Uncle's daydream, not a claim about the real festival.
- "Irasshaimase" gag: the joke is the Uncle thinking it's personal. Chef Taka says it warmly; never make the greeting itself the punchline.
- Chef Taka is a fictional character. His shop has no name on screen and no real signage.
- Continuity: the guestbook note ("keep my pan safe") sets up Episode 6, where Chef Taka gives the Boy Dad's old pan.
