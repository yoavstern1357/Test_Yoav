A food-travel show on the old Silk Road, hosted by a boy and (mostly) interrupted by his uncle. We follow Dad's old dumpling journal, one dumpling per page.
Stop 2: Guangzhou, the dim sum city, and har gow: the see-through shrimp dumpling.

In 2026, Guangzhou adopted official rules to protect its morning-tea tradition (yum cha means "drink tea"). Among them: dim sum must be served within 24 hours of making.
Silk Road note: Guangzhou has been a trading port for more than 2,000 years. A king's tomb found there in the 1980s held African ivory, frankincense and a silver box in a Persian style (where the box was made is debated). Experts read these finds as signs of early sea trade.
