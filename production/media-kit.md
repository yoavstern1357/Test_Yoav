## The story
A curious boy in a conical hat and his big, always-hungry uncle follow an old, hand-drawn dumpling journal around the world: Shanghai, Tokyo, Seoul, Kathmandu, Mumbai, Kayseri, Tbilisi, Kraków, Bologna, Buenos Aires, and a season finale in Vietnam. In every country they taste a dumpling, learn the real history and culture behind it, cook a simple family recipe, and collect one clue to a secret recipe.

**A 6-year-old laughs at the Uncle, and a 40-year-old learns something.** Soft, warm animation; respectful of every culture; the jokes are always on the Uncle.

## Formats
| Format | Length | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrival Short** | 45–60s vertical | Travel to a new country, meet the dumpling, the Uncle's gag, the "Legend says…" history moment |
| **Culture Short** | 45–60s vertical | How locals really eat it, a friendly local character, a journal clue |
| **Recipe episode** | 3–5 min horizontal + 60s Short cut | A simple family recipe with "kid job" and "grown-up step" markers, plus a Recap card and map stamp |
| **Specials** | 5–12 min | Holiday episodes (e.g. Lunar New Year), compilations, season finale |

All formats are cross-posted natively to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

## Example integrations that fit the show
- **"Uncle's Kitchen Tool"**: the Uncle uses your bamboo steamer, dumpling press or non-stick pan in the recipe episode (organic, 15–30s, shown in action).
- **Ingredient partner**: your wrappers, flour, sauces or frozen dumplings as the "shortcut" option in the recipe ("No time to fold? Here's the Uncle's secret").
- **"Supported by" card**: a short spoken thank-you plus logo at the start of a recipe episode or an entire country arc (3 episodes).
- **Recap card / Dumpling Passport sponsor**: your logo on the end-of-arc Recap card and on the printable "Dumpling Passport" families download and color.
- **Travel & culture partner**: family travel, language-learning or world-food delivery brands tied to the country of the arc.
- **Custom animated Short**: a stand-alone story Short built around your product, in the show's style (subject to creative fit).

Brand fit: food, cooking, kitchenware, groceries, family travel, education and culture. We don't promote products that are unhealthy for kids to over-consume, unsafe for children, or that mock any culture. Every partnership is clearly disclosed in the video, in the description and with YouTube's "paid promotion" label, in words children can understand where required.
