## Episode 03: Recipe (food safety)

| Claim / instruction | Verdict | Notes | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 packet gelatin = "7 g / about 2¼ tsp" | ❌ fixed → "about 2½ tsp" | Knox lists one 7 g envelope as about 2½ tsp. | https://knoxgelatine.com/about-us · https://knoxgelatine.com/basics_Bot.htm |
| 1 packet per 1 cup broth, chilled about 3 h, firm enough to cube | ✅ | One envelope softly sets 2 cups and firmly sets up to about 1½ cups, so 1 cup gives a firm, cuttable jelly (about double the standard strength). 3 h to overnight is realistic. | Knox pages above · https://www.davidlebovitz.com/how-to-use-gelatin/ |
| Bloom in cold broth, warm gently, don't boil | ✅ | Standard method. | Knox pages above |
| "1 heaped teaspoon of filling" per wrapper | ❌ fixed → "about 1 tablespoon" | 250 g pork plus about 250 g jelly is about 500 g over 24 wrappers, roughly 20 g each. A heaped teaspoon is 5–8 g, so the filling wouldn't match 24 wrappers. (Din Tai Fung uses 16 g of filling per dumpling.) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Din_Tai_Fung |
| Steam 8–10 min "until the filling is fully cooked" | ⚠️ clarified | 8–10 min is typical for small dumplings on thin wrappers (other recipes say up to 12–15 min). The script gave no way to check doneness. Added a grown-up check: no pink, or a thermometer at **160°F/71°C for ground pork** or **165°F/74°C for ground chicken**, plus "steam 2–3 more minutes" if needed. | https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart · https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures · https://thewoksoflife.com/steamed-shanghai-soup-dumplings-xiaolongbao/ |

| Grown-up step markers | ⚠️ added | Added "open the steamer lid away from your face" (steam burn). | general steam-burn guidance |
| Hot soup burn risk for kids | ❌ fixed (added) | Added: wait 2–3 minutes after steaming, never eat one whole like the Uncle, and for little kids a grown-up opens the dumpling on the spoon and lets the soup cool first. | Shanghai gov "lift gently, move slowly, make a small opening, then sip" |

## Recommended changes NOT made (and why)

1. **Gyoza → Utsunomiya (or Tokyo + Utsunomiya day trip); Empanada → Tucumán/Famaillá or Salta; Vietnam → Huế; Khinkali → Tbilisi + Pasanauri.** These are creative and story decisions for the showrunner, and episodes 4+ are being written now. The current pairings aren't wrong, only less specific.
2. **Kimono + nón lá costume (CHANNEL_KIT, character bible).** A Japanese kimono with a Vietnamese hat may read as mixing up East Asian cultures, and for a show whose rule is "locals should feel proud" that is a risk. Consider a Vietnamese outfit (áo bà ba or a simple áo dài-style top), or describe it as a "white wrap jacket" rather than "kimono". Not changed because it's a design decision and the art may already exist.
3. **Episode 01 gag (Uncle swallows a hot soup dumpling whole).** It's fine as slapstick, but kids copy behavior. Consider a 2–3 s beat where the Boy shows the spoon method (a Canva "right way (spoon)" image already exists but isn't in the script), so Episode 03's "callback to Episode 1" works. Not changed because it means re-timing the 60 s script.
4. **"Turkey" → "Türkiye"** in the season map. This is the country's official UN name since 2022. Either is acceptable in English, so it's a style call.
5. **Donation calls in descriptions ("Support the journey").** If videos are set as made for kids, aim the calls at parents ("Grown-ups: support the journey…"). Google's ad policies discourage urging children to buy things. Not changed because it's a copy-tone choice across many files.
6. **Episode 03 timing.** If the long video is set as made for kids, the description chapters and links still work, but the end screens/cards listed in the teaser ("subscribe and Ko-fi reminder") won't be clickable. Plan the verbal call to action instead.
7. **The aspic ratio could be noted in a "tip".** Using rich homemade stock (or adding a pork-skin aspic) is more traditional than powdered gelatin. Optional, so not added to keep the recipe supermarket-simple.
8. **Episode 04 shows uncommitted changes in git** from another writer. I did not touch episodes 04–09.
