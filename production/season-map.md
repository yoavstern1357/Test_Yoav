# Season Map: The Dumpling Boy (Silk Road route)

Planned: 2026-11-24 · Replaces the old 11-country "Season 1 map" in `series-format.md`.
**39 episodes in 13 arcs:** Season 1 = 21 episodes (7 arcs), Season 2 = 18 episodes (6 arcs). Every episode is ~2:00 and about **one** dumpling. Every 3rd episode is **Recipe + Recap**.

## 1. Why this route

**Two Silk Roads, two seasons.** The Silk Road was a web of routes, not one road. Its two big branches make a clean season split:

- **Season 1: the Maritime Silk Road (by sea).** It starts in China's great ports: Shanghai, then **Guangzhou**, one of the oldest sea-trade ports on the route. It goes north-east to Korea and Japan along the old East Asian sea lanes, then south through the Philippines (Manila galleon trade, Chinese settlements like Binondo and Molo), Vietnam (Hội An was a major international trading port), Thailand, and the **Strait of Malacca**, the busiest chokepoint of the sea road. The season ends in India, the classic western end of the Asian sea route. This leg is all ports and ships: boats, ferries, harbors.
- **Season 2: the Land Silk Road and Steppe Road (by caravan and train).** Season 2 follows Marco Polo's world **backwards to his home**. It starts in Mongolia, which ruled the routes in the 13th century: Marco Polo travelled to Kublai Khan's court, and the Mongol capital Karakorum is where travellers from east and west met. The road goes west along the steppe and the Tea Road (Mongolia → Siberia), down through the Central Asian "-stans" (Almaty, the Kyrgyz mountains, Samarkand, Khiva), across the Caspian to the Caucasus, through Anatolia (Türkiye) and its caravan cities, and by sea to Italy. The finale is in **Venice**, Marco Polo's hometown.

**Fixes to the creator's rough list, and why:**
| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Korea added**, placed China → Korea → Japan | Korea sits between China and Japan, and its 3 existing scripts are reused. Busan → Japan by ferry is a real route. |
| **Mongolia and Russia moved to Season 2** | They're on the land/steppe route, not the sea route. Mongolia opens Season 2 as Marco Polo's "Mongol world". |
| **Philippines before Vietnam** | Ships from Japan sail south to Luzon first. From Manila/Iloilo it's a short hop west to Vietnam. |
| **Malaysia (Malacca) + Singapore added** (with Thailand) | The Strait of Malacca is the heart of the Maritime Silk Road. They also give the single-dumpling countries a 3-episode "Straits arc". |
| **Nepal/Himalaya handled in the India arc** (Darjeeling momo) | It keeps India at 3 episodes and ends Season 1 at the mountains, where the land road begins. Option: swap Ep 21 to **Kathmandu, Nepal** (see notes). |
| **Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan** for the "-stans" | They're on the main caravan line, and Samarkand and Khiva are the iconic Silk Road cities. |
| **Caucasus = Azerbaijan, Georgia, Armenia** (1 episode each) | Each has one iconic dumpling, so they form a 3-country region arc on the way from the Caspian to Anatolia. |
| **Afghanistan not visited** | It's historically on the route, and mantu/ashak are great dumplings, but travel content there is currently sensitive for a family show. Mention it on the map only ("Dad wrote: 'Afghanistan: ashak with mint, someday'"). |
| **Poland and Argentina dropped** | They aren't on the Silk Road. Keep them for a possible "Season 3: beyond the Silk Road". |
| **Finale moves from Huế to Venice** | Huế stays as Dad's big emotional beat (where he got the hat), at the end of the Vietnam arc. |
