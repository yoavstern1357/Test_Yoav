# Series structure plan v5: The Dumpling Boy (for creator approval, no scripts yet)

Written by: showrunner / head writer. Date: 2026-11-26. Status: **PROPOSAL**. Nothing here changes a script until the creator approves it (decisions list in section 7).
Basis: the **1-minute format** ("FORMAT CHANGE: ~1-MINUTE EPISODES" at the end of `PRODUCTION-BIBLE.md`): 1:00 target, 1:15 hard max, hook first, no separate opening, 150-170 words, 20-26 clips of 2-3 s, max 2 faces per clip, recipe every 3rd episode.
Read with: `season-map.md` (route and dumplings, kept), `series-format.md` (format this plan replaces), `comedy-style.md` (comedy rules, kept), `fix-plan.md` Fix 2 (shot rules, kept), `marketing.md` 2.2-2.5 (package and binge hooks).

## 0. The plan in ten lines
1. **Keep the route and the 39 dumplings.** Change how they are grouped: **4 short seasons** instead of 2 long ones. **Season 1 = 12 one-minute episodes** (China, Korea, Japan, Philippines). It runs 6 weeks at 2 episodes a week and ends on a real payoff.
2. **Season question:** *Where is the torn last page of Dad's journal, and what is Dad's Dumpling?* **Answer in the S1 finale:** the Uncle has carried half of the page, unopened, the whole time.
3. **Stakes come from people, not from danger.** The Boy wants to finish Dad's recipe. The Uncle hides a secret: he started this trip with Dad years ago and went home after a quarrel. A **rival host** chases the same "Leaf Hat Recipe" for her show.
4. **Each episode works alone** (one dumpling, one question, answered in the minute) and carries **exactly one serial beat** (Dad, rival, or the Uncle's secret).
5. Every episode ends with a **next-episode hook**, then "Dad was right...", then a 1 s camel sting that loops back to frame 1.
6. **3-episode country arcs stay:** Arrival (legend) -> Culture (thread beat) -> Recipe + Recap (stamp). Three episodes join into a **~3 min 16:9 compilation** with the full opening song.
7. **Running gags escalate across the season** and pay off a character arc. The main one: the Uncle's "sock" dumplings get better until he folds a real one in the finale.
8. **Recurring cast:** the Narrator, the Camel, Grandma's ice pack, the rival and her producer, and locals who recognize the hat. Teachers return at season finales.
9. **Midseason twist (Ep 6):** a photo shows two silhouettes at Dad's table. **Finale (Ep 12):** the Uncle hands over the half page. **Series finale:** Venice.
10. **Produce first:** cut Ep 1 to 60 s, design the rival, then script Ep 2-6. Bank 6 episodes before launch.

## 1. Audit of the current series format

### 1.1 What works (keep)
| Element | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **One dumpling, one place, one question per episode** | Clear promise. Every episode can be someone's first, which Shorts need. |
| **Dad's journal as the spine** | A ready-made route, an emotional engine, and a reason for the next place. "Dad was right..." is a brandable sign-off. |
| **3-episode country arcs** (Arrival / Culture / Recipe + Recap) | Rhythm the audience learns. A natural unit for compilations and playlists. |
| **Comedy engine** (manic Uncle vs deadpan Boy vs epic Narrator, deadpan local master) | Proven in the v4.4 script. Works muted (over half the laughs are visual). |
| **Accuracy discipline** ("Legend says", 2 sources, the Boy's "historian for please don't quote me") | Builds trust with adults and turns hedging into a joke. |
| **Silk Road route split by sea and land, with the Venice / Marco Polo myth-buster finale** | A strong, true endpoint with an "aha" (Marco Polo did not bring pasta). |
| **Ending every episode on the next place** | Already a basic binge hook. |

## 3. Proposed series architecture

### 3.1 Season size: recommendation
| Option | Shape | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep 2 seasons (21 + 18) | S1 = all of the sea road | Matches `season-map.md` | 21 one-minute episodes = 10.5 weeks before the first real payoff. Too long a promise for a new channel. |
| **B. 4 seasons (12 + 9 + 9 + 9) = 39 [RECOMMENDED]** | S1 "The Eastern Sea": China, Korea, Japan, Philippines (Ep 1-12). S2 "The Southern Sea": Vietnam, Straits, India (13-21). S3 "The Steppe and the Sands": Mongolia, Tea Road and Steppe, Uzbekistan (22-30). S4 "The Road West": Caucasus, Türkiye, Italy (31-39). | A finale every 5-6 weeks at 2/week. S1 is ready for the Shorts series feature. After the 10-Short review rule in `marketing.md` 2.3, there is a natural break to adjust before S2. Same 39 dumplings. | More finales to write (good for retention). |
| C. 4-episode arcs | More depth per country | | Breaks the 3-episode compilation and the recipe-every-3rd rhythm. Not needed at 60 s. |

**Why 12 for S1:** 4 arcs = 4 compilations (about 3 min each, 16:9, full opening song) = about 12 min of long-form for watch hours. The Philippines arc ends the season on the sea road just before **Vietnam (Dad's arc)**, the strongest cliffhanger in the route. 12 episodes at 2 per week = **6 weeks**, or 12 weeks at 1 per week if production falls behind. Global numbering 1-39 stays; "S1E7" is shown in the Shorts series only.

**Cadence:** 2 episodes a week (for example Tue and Fri), each arc's compilation the following Sunday. Bank **6 episodes (2 arcs) before launch** so a bad week does not break the cadence.

**GPU check (estimate, from `fix-plan.md` measured basis):** about 24 clips x 1.3 (re-rolls) at about 1.0-1.5 GPU-min each (576x1024 close-ups) is about 30-45 GPU-min. Add about 50 stills x 2 variants (about 20 min), voices and mouth pass (about 0.5-1 h). That is roughly **1.5-2.5 GPU-h per episode, so about 5 GPU-h a week for 2 episodes**, well under the 22 h stop line. The real bottleneck is review time (stills gate, clip QC, the creator's approval), not GPU.

### 3.2 Season question and stakes
- **Series question (all 4 seasons):** *What is Dad's Dumpling, and can the Boy and the Uncle cook it together?* Answered in Ep 39, Venice.
- **Season 1 question:** *Where is the torn last page?* Planted in Ep 1 (the torn edge, 1 s, with the Boy's line "The last page is torn out"). Escalated by the rival (Ep 2), the photo (Ep 6) and the confession (Ep 8). **Answered in Ep 12:** the Uncle has carried **half** the page, folded and unopened, since Dad mailed it to him. The half page holds the first half of the recipe and one line: *"Finish it with my brother. The rest is where Marco Polo began."* It also has a drawing of a conical hat with a poem in the brim. That opens S2 (Huế, the hat) and the series endpoint (Venice).
- **What Dad's journal is really leading to:** Dad was not collecting recipes. He was building **one** dumpling from everything the road taught him, to cook with his brother. The trip is the recipe. The torn page is torn because the two brothers each kept a half.
- **Stakes, kid-safe:**
  1. **Trust (internal, main):** the Boy learns the Uncle hid that he was on the first half of Dad's trip. Can they still be a team? (S1, resolved warmly in S2 at Huế.)
  2. **The race (external, comic):** the rival host wants to reveal the "Leaf Hat Recipe" on her show first. If she gets it, Dad's dumpling becomes a 10-second clip with a sponsor jingle instead of a family meal.
  3. **Loss (quiet, never milked):** when the journal ends, the last thing Dad left ends too. The Boy slows down near the end of each season. The Uncle notices.
- **Dad's status rule:** Dad is in the past tense, in sepia, from behind. S1 never says "died". Kids read "gone", adults understand. Huế (S2, Ep 15) is the one place it is said gently ("He wanted to come back here. He couldn't."). No jokes near it, ever (comedy-style ban 7).

### 3.8 Twists and finale payoffs

- **S1 finale (Ep 12, Iloilo):** the Uncle takes the folded half page from his jacket, unopened for years. Two lines: "He sent it to me. I couldn't open it." They open it **together**: the first half of the recipe, the hat drawing, *"Finish it with my brother. The rest is where Marco Polo began."* Pepper films from afar, then lowers the camera. The lower third reads "(approved)". The Boy says "For dumpling research." Boat to Vietnam.
- **S2 heart (Ep 15, Huế):** the hat maker reads the poem hidden in the brim: Dad's message to the Boy. The one place Dad's absence is said gently. The Uncle eats slowly.
- **S2 finale (Ep 21, Darjeeling):** the journal's back cover unfolds into the land-road map. Train whistle to Mongolia.
- **S3 finale (Ep 30, Khiva):** a caravanserai guestbook: Dad's last signature, *"Venice, with my brother, someday."*
- **Series finale (Ep 39, Venice):** the second half of the page (in Dad's favourite café, or the journal's spine). All teachers return (a cameo collage). The Uncle cooks and the Boy eats first. Pepper films but does not post. The Marco Polo pasta myth-buster. The last page is written by the Boy. The final image: the hat on a gondola at sunset.

### 6.1 Sensitivity risks
| Risk | Where | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Grief in a family show | Ep 1-15, 39 | Never blunt in S1. Huế is told gently, with no jokes within 10 s of a Dad beat. Optional: one read by a parent or child-media adviser before Ep 15. |
| The Uncle's secret reads as abandonment | Ep 6-12 | Make it a **quarrel between brothers**, which kids understand, with no villain. Dad's postcard shows Dad forgave him first. Resolve within the same season. |
| The rival parodies real people | All R beats | No real platform, brand, app or creator look-alike. Mock the *behaviour* ("content first, taste later"), never kids who make videos. Pepper is never humiliated; the Uncle stays the butt. |
| Country / culture framing | Arcs 2, 4, 5, 6 | Existing bans (`comedy-style.md` 6, `season-map.md` respect flags): no "too spicy / weird food" jokes (Ep 4); no Opium War / Canton system / Hong Kong ranking (Ep 2); Xinjiang = archaeology only (Ep 3); Ep 8 postwar history = say "came from northern China's jiaozi", without repatriation politics; Philippines colonial history = trade and food only (Ep 10-11); **Thailand: no royal jokes and no royal names, "palace cooks of long ago"** (Ep 16); modak offering respect (Ep 19); the Caucasus as three separate countries, no rivalry framing (31-33). |
| Kids copying | Burn, frying, egg | The safe version on screen in the same minute. GROWN-UP STEP badges. Doneness temperatures. Allergy line every recipe (shrimp in Ep 2, wheat, soy, egg). |

### 6.2 Factual risks at 60 s
- Compression turns hedges into claims. Rule: a legend gets the spoken words "Legend says" or "Many people believe" **every time**, even in 8 s. The nuance goes in the description and sources.
- One to two facts per episode only, tagged FIRM in `content-research.md` style. Disputed numbers (fold counts, dates) are said as "cooks disagree" or dropped.
- Flags still open from `season-map.md`: Ep 3 Beijing as setting, not birthplace; Ep 23 and 27 local ties; Ep 21 India or Nepal; Ep 10 crust details (verify). Each script re-checks 2 sources.

### 6.3 Production feasibility (free pipeline, `fix-plan.md` Fix 2)

| Fix 2.7 risky shot | Where the old format had it | v5 staging |
|---|---|---|
| Flying or rocketing characters | Ep 1 burn rocket | Impact frame + speed lines (code), then **cut** to the barrel |
