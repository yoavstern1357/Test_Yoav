## 0. The plan in ten lines **[v6]**
1. **Route and dumplings kept, grouped into 4 short seasons: 12 / 9 / 9 / 9 story episodes** (39 story uploads: Ep 1-39). Season 1 = China, Korea, Japan, Philippines.
2. **Season question (all of S1):** *Dad vanished and left his dumpling book; the Boy believes the answers are in the book. What is Dad's Dumpling, and where is Dad?* **There is no Uncle secret, no torn page and no quarrel.** S1 answers the first half partly and gently (section 3.8): Dad's Dumpling is not one secret ingredient but the lessons of the road, and the S1 stamps already spell Dad's message. Dad's fate is never stated in S1; one gentle beat comes at Huế (S2).
3. **Stakes come from people and play, not from danger:** the Boy wants to finish Dad's recipe; the Uncle wants to be a real co-host (his arc: from eating to folding); **recurring friendly cook-off / taste BATTLES** (a 15-20 s repeatable beat, section 3.9) fill the middle; a **rival host (Pepper) arrives only at Ep 6** and returns about 1 episode in 3.
4. **Each story episode works alone** (one dumpling, one question, answered in the minute) and carries **exactly one serial beat** (Dad/book, or rival). Battles are the format, not a serial beat.
5. Every episode ends with a **next-episode hook**, then "Dad was right...", then a 1 s camel sting that loops back to frame 1.
6. **3-episode country arcs stay** (S1: China = Ep 1, 2, 3; Korea = 4, 5, 6; Japan = 7, 8, 9; Philippines = 10, 11, 12). Each arc ends with a **stamp beat** inside its 3rd story episode. A **~3 min 16:9 compilation** (full opening song) is an optional bonus per arc.
7. **Recipe is a separate 60-s Short after every 2 story episodes, about a NEW (bonus) dumpling** (section 3.10): S1 = 12 story + 6 recipe = **18 uploads = 18 weeks at 1 per week, 17 dumplings**.
8. **Running gags escalate** and pay off the Uncle's arc: his "sock" dumplings get better until he folds a real one in the S1 finale (Ep 12).
9. **Recurring cast:** the Narrator, the Camel, Grandma's ice pack, the rival and her producer, and local masters who recognise the hat ("Dad's Leaf Hat friends").
10. **Produce first:** Ep 1, 2, recipe R1, Ep 3, 4, recipe R2 = the **6-upload bank** before launch (script of each approved in Hebrew in chat first).

## 1. Audit of the current series format

### 1.1 What works (keep)
| Element | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **One dumpling, one place, one question per episode** | Clear promise. Every episode can be someone's first, which Shorts need. |

| **Ending every episode on the next place** | Already a basic binge hook. |

## 3. Series architecture **[v6: final]**

### 3.1 Season size and cadence
| Option | Shape | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A. 2 seasons (21 + 18) | S1 = the whole sea road | Rejected: too long a promise for a new channel. |
| **B. 4 seasons (12 + 9 + 9 + 9) = 39 story uploads [APPROVED]** | **S1 "The Eastern Sea"**: China, Korea, Japan, Philippines (Ep 1-12). **S2 "The Southern Sea"**: Vietnam, the Straits, India (Ep 13-21). **S3 "The Steppe and the Sands"**: Mongolia, Tea Road and Steppe, Uzbekistan (Ep 22-30). **S4 "The Road West"**: Caucasus, Türkiye, Italy (Ep 31-39). | A finale every 13-18 weeks, each with a real payoff. |
| C. 4-episode arcs | | Rejected: breaks the 3-episode arc and the stamp rhythm. |

**[v6] Numbering.** **[Creator decision 2026-11-28: plain consecutive numbers, no "1A/1B".]** The 12 in S1 counts **Ep 1 and Ep 2** (the old Ep 1 is split into two episodes). That makes 39 numbered story episodes (Ep 1-39), 38 of them with a new dumpling (Ep 2 has none). The old Ep 3 (Beijing guotie) is **no longer a story episode**: it becomes the first recipe Short (R1). So the old route shifts like this: old Ep 1 -> Ep 1 + Ep 2; old Ep 2 (Guangzhou) -> Ep 3; **old Ep n keeps its number for every n >= 4**, so the original season-map numbering returns (Seoul is now Ep 4, Molo is Ep 12, Huế is Ep 15, Venice is Ep 39). No other dumpling is dropped and no city is added. Details: top of `season-map.md`.

**Why 12 for S1:** 4 arcs of 3 = 4 optional compilations (about 3 min, 16:9, full opening song). The Philippines arc ends the season on the sea road just before **Vietnam (the hat's home)**, the strongest cliffhanger in the route.

**Cadence [v6]: 1 upload per week** (a single fixed slot, for example Saturday morning in the main audience's time zone; the creator may move it). Story episodes and recipe Shorts share this one slot (pattern: story, story, recipe). **Bank rule: 6 finished uploads before launch** (weeks 1-6: Ep 1, 2, R1, Ep 3, Ep 4, R2), and never fewer than 4 banked ahead during the run. **Upgrade rule:** only if the bank stays at 6 or more for 4 weeks in a row, add a second weekly upload (a story episode, never two recipes in a row). Compilations are optional bonuses outside the weekly promise.

**What 1 per week means:** S1 = 12 story + 6 recipe = **18 uploads = 18 weeks (about 4 months)**, **11 story dumplings + 6 bonus recipe dumplings = 17 dumplings** (Ep 2 has no new dumpling). The whole series = 39 story + 18 recipe = **57 uploads (about 13 months)** and about **56 dumplings** (38 story + 18 recipe; plus one optional post-finale "Dad's Dumpling" recipe Short). v5 at 2 per week was 6 weeks for the same 12 episodes: the calendar is 3 times longer, but each week carries one tested, banked upload.

**GPU check (estimate, from the `fix-plan.md` measured basis):** a story episode is about 1.5-2.5 GPU-h (about 24 clips x 1.3 re-rolls, stills x 2 variants, voices, mouth pass); a recipe Short is cheaper, about 1 GPU-h (mostly faceless hands-and-food clips). S1 is about **30-40 GPU-h in total, so about 2 GPU-h a week**, far below the 22 h stop line. The bottleneck is review time (the creator reads each script in Hebrew first), not GPU.

### 3.2 Season question and stakes **[v6: rewritten]**
- **Series and Season 1 question:** *Dad vanished and left his dumpling book; the Boy believes the answers are in the book. What is Dad's Dumpling, and where is Dad?* Planted in **Ep 2** (Dad's silhouette, the book, the first page in his hand). Answered in steps: S1 gives the first answers (3.8), S2 the one gentle Dad beat (Huế), S4 the full dumpling in Venice (Ep 39).
- **No torn page, no Uncle secret, no quarrel.** The book is complete and written to the Boy. The recipe is not hidden in one place: it is spread across the stamps, one lesson per country. The last page is **blank on purpose** (Dad left room for the Boy to write it).
- **Stakes, kid-safe:**
  1. **The book (quiet, main):** it is the last thing Dad left. The Boy believes the answers are in it. The Boy slows down near the end of each season; the Uncle notices.
  2. **The race (comic):** from Ep 6 a rival host wants the "Leaf Hat Recipe" first, for her show. If she gets it, Dad's dumpling becomes a 10-second clip with a sponsor jingle instead of a family meal.
  3. **The game (light):** in most episodes a friendly **battle** (3.9) gives a small win, loss or draw, always with a lesson.
- **Dad's status rule:** Dad **vanished**. In the story he is in the past, in sepia, from behind. **S1 never says what happened to him**, and never says "died". The Boy's own line is calm and hopeful ("Dad went away and left me his book. The answers must be in it."). **Huế (S2, Ep 15) is the one place his absence is touched, gently**, with a line such as "He said he would come back when the dumpling was finished." (draft; the creator approves the final words in Hebrew). No jokes within 10 s of any Dad beat (`comedy-style.md` ban 7). No search party, no villain, no menace.

### 3.9 The BATTLE format (repeatable 15-20 s beat) **[v6: new]**
Working name: **"The Bell Round"**. A friendly dumpling cook-off or **taste** battle that **replaces the central-gag slot** (about 0:22-0:40) on battle episodes. It turns the Uncle's hubris into a small game with a clear winner and one concrete lesson.

**The four beats (15-20 s, about 6-8 clips, max 2 faces per clip; two-contestant shots are split-screen composited in code):**
| Beat | Time | What | Sound / overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. CALL | 3 s | The local master rings a small brass bell (or taps a spoon on a steamer lid). A 1 s "ROUND n" card with 3 icons: what is judged (Look, Texture, Taste) and the sand-timer | Bell, then a tick |
| 2. GO | 7-8 s | Shot / reverse shot or split-screen: the Uncle's boast + hurry (the gag) against the opponent's calm skill. One physical gag only | Ticking, kitchen sounds |
| 3. JUDGE | 4-5 s | The master looks, touches, tastes, and says one short line. The scoreboard (3 star rows, code overlay) fills in | Soft chime per star |
| 4. RESULT | 3 s | Winner, draw or loss, a bow or handshake, and **the lesson in one line** (it is the episode's learning payoff) | Bell, short sting |

**Round types (rotate; never the same type twice in a row):**
| Type | What it is | S1 use |
|---|---|---|
| **Fold-off** | Same wrappers and filling, judged on seal and look | Ep 3 (the Uncle vs the Boy, judge Auntie Lan) |
| **Taste-ID** | Name each filling by smell and one small bite (equal portions) | Ep 4 (market cook) |
| **Team relay** | Two teams, each member folds a stage | Ep 5 (the kids vs the Uncle) |
| **Timing** | Judge doneness by sight, sound and smell, no peeking rule | Ep 7 (steam), the Uncle peeks |
| **Crisp / pan** | The same pan, who gets the better bottom | Ep 8 (friendly visiting cook) |
| **Shape / crimp** | A braided or pleated edge | Ep 10 |
| **Rival Round** | Teams of two, hosts vs hosts | Ep 9 (cook-off), Ep 11 (partner swap) |

**Rules for fairness and safety (apply to every battle):**
1. Same ingredients, same time, same tools for both sides. The criteria are shown before the round and the result refers to them.
2. A **local master judges**. The Uncle never judges, the audience never votes inside the episode, and nobody judges themselves.
3. **Result rotation:** over a season the Uncle loses about half, draws a quarter and wins a quarter, and he wins only on honest merit (his nose, his taste), never by luck played for laughs. Rival Rounds end as draws or shared wins until the finale.
4. **No humiliation of the opponent.** Never a joke about their skill, their looks, their age, their country or their people. The opponent always keeps dignity, gets a bow or handshake, and may be a recurring friend.
5. **Jokes only on the Uncle's behaviour** (boasting, hurrying, peeking, the sock), never on his weight.
6. The loser learns **one concrete technique** from the winner: the lesson is the learning beat. No punishments, no forfeits, no bets, no insults.
7. **Eating rounds are taste rounds:** small equal portions, no speed eating, no "most eaten" records, no swallowing races, no mess-making for laughs. Hot food is cooled first and the hot badge is on screen. Food is eaten or shared afterwards, never wasted.
8. The Boy never does a speed or quantity round. Knives and hot oil appear only with the GROWN-UP STEP badge and are handled by the local master.
9. **No real creator, platform, TV cooking competition or contest format is parodied** (no named catchphrases, jingles, set designs or UI). The bell, the card style and the star scoreboard are original.
10. Religious or ceremonial food (for example the modak offering, Ep 19) never gets a battle.

**Schedule:** battles in S1 on Ep 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10 (local) and Ep 9, 11 (Rival Rounds). No battle in Ep 1, 2 (setup), Ep 6 (the rival's arrival takes the slot) or Ep 12 (the finale is a cooperative cook). Later seasons: about 2 battles per 3-episode arc, never in a heart episode (Ep 15) or an offering episode (Ep 19).

### 3.10 Recipe Shorts **[v6: new]**
- **What:** a **separate 60-s Short after every 2 story episodes** (S1: weeks 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18), numbered **R1, R2...**, about a **NEW dumpling**. Not inside story episodes. Full written recipe, quantities, allergens and sources in the description.
- **Decision: bonus dumplings, not the arc's dumpling.** A recipe Short never repeats a dumpling already in a story episode. It is a **cousin** from the country of the story pair it follows (or the next one), so it feels connected without spoiling. Reasons: every recipe is a new search target; the story dumplings stay special; no repetition against the repetitive-content policy; more variety (savoury and sweet, steamed, boiled, fried); the S1 dumpling total grows from 11 to 17.
- **Standalone and spoiler-free:** a new viewer can start here. No serial beat, no stamp, no rival. Allowed: the Uncle's sock gag in the fold step, a Dad's-margin-note card, and the sign-off "Dad was right..." plus a text tease of the next story episode.
- **Set:** "Dad's recipe kitchen", a warm simple kitchen used every time (stove, wooden table, the journal open at a recipe card, a map pin of the dumpling's origin). Mostly **faceless hands-and-food clips** (the most reliable kind and the cheapest).
- **Safety (hard rules, from the bible):** GROWN-UP STEP badge on every knife, boiling water, hot oil and steam step; doneness 71 C / 160 F for pork, 74 C / 165 F for chicken (shown on screen and in the description); an **allergy line** (the common allergens in this recipe, plus substitutes); a substitute for pork where possible; raw-meat hygiene line; no copying the burn gag for kids. Chewy rice dumplings (R3, R4, R6) carry a **choking note**: serve small, sitting down, not for toddlers.
