# Attention and pace spec: ADAPTED pace for The Dumpling Boy

Written 2026-11-28 · Creator directive: "run research on viewer attention and, based on it, decide on the design, the flicker/rhythm, the pace, the timings and the sounds." Clarification the same day: the pace must be ADAPTED to the audience (families, adults and kids, mobile Shorts viewers, learning content), not as fast as possible. So the rule of this file is: **fast where it earns attention, medium where we talk, calm where comprehension or emotion needs it.** Applies to `scripts/episode-01-v5.md` and `scripts/episode-02-v5.md` now and to every later episode. Enforced by `tools/episode_maker/paper/lint_shotlist.py` (PACE / CUTS / TRIM / FLASH columns, section 9) and `tools/episode_maker/qc_flash.py` (rendered video, section 7).

## 0. The decisions in one screen
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Principle | Three pace modes per beat: **FAST** (hook, opening, gags, impacts, transitions), **MEDIUM** (host, place, banter, instructions with verbs), **CALM** (the one fact, the safety steps, the Dad beat, the last line). Pace follows comprehension and emotion; no beat is faster than its content can be understood. |
| Average shot length (ASL, counting edit cuts inside a clip) | Episode overall **1.2-1.7 s** for the premiere (its 11 s of hook + opening run at about 0.6 s), **1.5-2.2 s** for a normal episode. By mode: FAST 0.4-1.2 s, MEDIUM 1.5-2.5 s, CALM 3.0-5.0 s. |
| Floors | No shot shorter than 0.25 s (6 frames at 24 fps) except impact frames (<= 2 frames). A new face or new text stays >= 0.5 s. A place card >= 1.6 s, the fact card >= 2.5 s and <= 5 words. |
| Re-hook cadence | A perceptible change (cut, punch-in, card or caption pop, SFX accent, music hit) at least every **2.0 s** in FAST/MEDIUM and every **2.5-3.0 s** in CALM (a micro-motion: slow push, particles, light, sound texture). A "big" re-hook (new place, gag, reveal, question) every **7 s +/- 2** outside the Dad beat. |
| Where the AI clips are cut | AI clips are generated at 2.0-3.5 s (their sweet spot, `motion-tech.md`) and **trimmed in the edit** (`TRIM a-b`, hot window only) and **cut into sub-shots in code** (`CUTS`): zoom punches, crops, inserts, sprites, card pops. Never ask the model for sub-second clips. |
| Sound | Underscore 90 BPM (calm) / 120 BPM (medium, fast gags) on the same grid as the 180 BPM song sting (2:1 and 3:2 relations); **a sound on every picture cut** (within +/- 40 ms); voice -14 LUFS programme, music ducked 12-14 dB under voice; **one** deliberate silence per episode; final master -14 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1 dBTP. |
| Flicker | Hard limit **<= 3 flashes in any 1 s** (we aim for <= 2), **<= 2-frame** impact frames with a luminance delta <= 0.30, **no red flashes**, no stripe or checker patterns, bright/dark alternation between cuts never faster than 3 per second. Checked on every render with `qc_flash.py`. |
| Captions | Word-pop, 1-3 words per pop, in step with the voice (about 2.2 words/s), cream text with brown outline, current word in leaf yellow, bottom edge <= y 1440 (480 px clear below), centre of mass at y 1150-1350, never over faces or hands. |

## 1. Research: what the evidence says (honest version)
How this was done: web search (summaries); the host session could not open w3.org, itu.int or pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov pages directly (network allowlist), so every number below comes from the search summaries of those sources, not from reading the full standard or paper. Where a number matters for safety, the full document must be re-read once (marked **RE-READ**). Evidence strength: **P** = primary study or standard, **R** = review / meta-analysis, **I** = industry report or vendor data, **O** = practitioner opinion or blog, **W** = news / encyclopedia summary.

### 1.5 What the evidence cannot tell us (so we measure)
- No source gives a validated ASL, cuts-per-minute, or re-hook interval for a 60-s animated educational Short aimed at families. Our numbers are reasoned design choices that stay inside three bounds: what research shows hurts (flash limits; comprehension lost when beats are too dense), what practitioners report works (hook in 1-3 s, regular changes), and what craft convention does (anime openings are dense; calm beats breathe).
- After upload, the creator's own YouTube Studio data decide: "viewed vs swiped away" for the hook, and the retention curve for 0:04-0:11 of Episode 1, 0:25-0:51 of Episode 2. If a mode misses, change the numbers in section 2 and re-lint; nothing else needs to change.
- ASD/ADHD: no pace study for animated Shorts exists; we only take sound-sensitivity and flash safety precautions (sections 6 and 7) and the calm beats as breathers.

## 2. Pace modes and numbers
| Mode | Used for | Picture rhythm | Re-hook | Sound | Reason (evidence #) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FAST** | hook 0-3.5 s, opening, gags and their payoff, impacts, chases, the catchphrase, transitions into a new beat | shots 0.4-1.2 s (median about 0.8); cut on action; punch / insert / sprite hits; an impact frame only at the biggest peak | visual or audio change <= every 1.2 s | a hit, whoosh or tick on every cut; music 120 BPM or the 180 BPM sting | first seconds decide swipe-away (9, 10); synchrony (12); arousal and a short-lived peak; flash-safe (20, 21) |
| **MEDIUM** | host and place, banter, the instruction steps (one verb per cut), reaction answers | shots 1.5-2.5 s; one punch-in or insert per line; cards pop | change <= every 2.0 s | underscore 120 BPM, SFX on cuts and on card pops | conversation needs readable faces and speech; wave pattern (7) |
| **CALM** | the one fact (soup goes in solid), the safe-eating steps, the payoff, the Dad beat, the last line, the cliffhanger | shots 3.0-5.0 s (AI clip max 3.5); no cuts, or one slow punch; micro-motion every 2.5-3.0 s (slow push 1.00 to 1.05-1.10, steam, leaf, lantern flicker, page rustle) | micro change <= every 2.5-3.0 s | sparse: piano or pad, ambience bed, a single texture sound; ducking deeper | comprehension and memory for the central message (3, 4, 5); emotion needs time; breather against overload (13) |

Wave rule: never more than **12 s** of continuous FAST (the premiere is the one exception: hook plus opening = 11 s), and never more than **12 s** of continuous CALM outside the Dad beat. Between a FAST peak and a CALM beat put a MEDIUM beat or a 0.3-0.5 s breath so the change of speed is felt as rhythm, not a drop.

## 3. The rhythm map for a 60-75 s episode
Time is from the first frame. Peaks are the moments where picture, sound and joke land together.
| Window | What happens | Mode | Re-hooks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-1.0 s | first frame is the funniest or strangest image; sound at 0.0-0.1 s | FAST | frame 0, 0.5 s |
| 1.0-3.5 s | the hook line; **peak 1** on the stressed word | FAST | 1.0, 1.8, 2.5 |
| 3.5-11 s | premiere: the opening (**peak 2** at the first sting hit, **peak 3** at the chase, title bloom at 10) · other episodes: the 3-s sting plus a <= 2 s title hit | FAST | every 0.3-0.7 s (premiere) |
| 11-22 s | host, place, the question, first gag | MEDIUM with a gag peak at about 17.5-18.5 | every 2 s |
| 22-36 s | the fact (CALM), the reaction (MEDIUM), the hero macro (CALM) | CALM / MEDIUM | micro every 2.5 s, **peak 4** = the reaction at the end of the fact |
| 36-47 s | hubris, catchphrase, countdown, impact, aftermath | FAST | every 1-1.5 s; **peak 5 (the biggest)** at the impact at about 44-45 s; the deadpan "...do that." is the release |
| 47-57 s | the Uncle's line, the safe method | MEDIUM / CALM | every 2 s |
| 57-65 s | the legend scroll | MEDIUM | scene change every 1.5 s |
| 65-last | payoff, doodle, last line | CALM | one micro per 2.5 s, **peak 6** = "Dad was right..." and the smash cut |
Allowed **breath / silence moments: one per episode** (0.4-0.8 s where music and ambience fall to -30 dB, not to digital zero), placed right before the emotional line (Episode 1: before "Wait... that's Dad's doodle."; Episode 2: before "That... that hat."). The countdown stop ("Three... two...") is a 0.3-s comic silence and does not count as the breath.

## 4. Caption style and timing
- **Word-pop**: each spoken word appears on its voice onset (use the forced-alignment timestamps); 1-3 words per caption event, never more than 3 words ahead of the voice; the previous words stay for at most 0.4 s after the next pop; a pop is a scale 0.9 to 1.06 to 1.0 over 6 frames (80-120 ms), no flashing and no colour inversion.
- Reading load: 2.0-2.4 words/s (about 11-14 characters/s) at natural voice speed; never above 20 characters/s (evidence 14).
- Size and look (1080 x 1920): font size >= 72 px, weight bold or black, rounded sans; 2 lines at most, <= 22 characters per line; fill cream `#FFFAF0`, outline 8 px brown `#3B2A1A`, soft shadow 4 px; the word being spoken is leaf yellow `#ECC66A`; the stressed word of a gag is orange `#D9480F` with a 6-frame scale pop. Contrast ratio of text to outline >= 7:1.
- **Safe area (9:16)**: caption block bottom edge at y <= 1440 (at least 480 px clear below), centre of mass at y 1150-1350, x 90-900 (right gutter >= 180 px for the Shorts buttons); top cards (place, fact card, badge) in y 270-420; the Shorts top bar is about the first 250 px. Faces and hands must stay clear of the caption box: each shot declares TOP or BOTTOM in the Caption column and the renderer obeys it (bible directive 5).
- **16:9**: captions in the lower third, bottom edge y <= 972 (10 % margin), x 192-1728, font size >= 54 px; cards top-left/top-centre inside the same 10 % margin.
- Captions are never the only carrier of a joke or the fact: voice and picture carry it too (sound-off viewers get the caption, others the voice).
- Opening: the optional lyric translation is small (36 px), bottom band, 2 s per line, lowest priority.

## 5. Transition vocabulary (all in code or edit, never in an AI prompt)
Frame counts at 24 fps. Every transition below has a sound (section 6).
| Name | What it is | Frames | Use | Flash-safe note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hard cut on action | cut at the peak of a movement or the stressed syllable | 0 | default | match luminance of neighbours |
| Smash cut | cut from a calm held shot to a loud peak (or the reverse) with a sound that arrives 1-2 frames early | 0 | countdown to impact; "Dad was right..." to black | keep the loud side within the loudness limits (section 6) |
| Speed ramp | on-ones time ramp 100 % to 300 % to 100 % of an AI clip around the key action | 6-12 | chops, spoon lift, chase | interpolation not allowed (known RIFE stripe bug): drop frames on ones |
| Impact frame | one stylised frame (cream `#FFFAF0` wash or radial lines with a high-contrast drawing) | **<= 2** | once per episode at the biggest peak, once at the title bloom in the premiere | luminance delta <= 0.30, area may be full, no red, never two within 0.5 s |
| Zoom punch | crop-scale 1.00 to 1.12 in 4 frames (ease-out), hold, ease back in 8 | 4 + 8 | stressed word, reveal, reaction | a cut-like change; the next punch >= 0.5 s later |
| Shake | damped sine 12-15 Hz, amplitude <= 6 px at 1080 w (<= 3 px in CALM), decays over <= 8 frames | <= 8 | impacts, chops, the burn | no shake longer than 0.4 s |
| Insert / crop | a 0.4-1.0 s detail cut (the eye twitch, the ice pack, the gyoza star), from the same clip's frames or a code sprite | 10-24 | gag set-ups | |
| Whip / scroll wipe | the Narrator scroll-edge wipe, 0.4 s | 10 | Narrator entrance (signature) | opaque paper colours, no strobing |
| J-cut / L-cut | the next scene's sound starts 4-8 frames before the picture (J) or the previous sound stays after it (L) | 4-8 | place changes, the Dad beat | |
| Steam wipe | steam puff covers the frame and clears | 8-10 | soft scene exits in CALM beats | cream, low contrast |
Avoid: dissolves in FAST beats, spinning transitions, glitch/strobe transitions, and white-to-black alternation faster than 3 per second.

## 6. Sound design spec
**Tempo grid.** The opening sting (`production/v4/opening-song.md`) is 170-190 BPM; use **180 BPM** (beat 0.333 s, 8th 0.167 s) as the master grid and lock the sting to it (regenerate or time-stretch within 3 %). Underscore: **90 BPM** for CALM (exactly half-time, every beat falls on a sting beat), **120 BPM** for MEDIUM and gag beats (3:2 against the sting, a beat every 0.5 s). Cuts of FAST beats land on the 180 BPM 8th grid (+/- 1 frame); cuts of MEDIUM beats on 120 BPM beats or on the stressed syllable. **Key:** the generated sting has no fixed key in the brief ("major, bright"); measure its key once (chroma or by ear), write it into `opening-song.md`, and write the underscore in the same key (CALM beats in its relative minor or on the IV chord). Until measured, the synth defaults stay as they are in `synth.py`.
**Every cut has a sound** within +/- 40 ms (evidence 12):
| Event | Sound (existing in `synth.py` / `sfx_extra*.py` unless marked NEW) |
|---|---|
| hard cut, FAST | `tick` or `pop` (3-8 ms attack), alternate two variants so it does not become a metronome |
| punch-in / zoom punch | `whoosh` 0.15 s + a soft `thud` (`sfx_extra.thud`) |
| smash cut | `impact` pre-cued by a 0.2-0.4 s `riser` or an inhale `whoosh` |
| impact frame | `impact` + `crack` + a low `gong` tail, peak <= voice +4 dB; **NEW** `sub_drop` (sine 55 to 35 Hz, 0.5 s, low-passed) |
| text / card pop | `pop` or `ding` (soft) |
| sprite pop (star eyes, sweat drop) | `sparkle` / `bloop` / `plink` |
| Narrator entrance | `gong` (existing) + scroll-edge `swoosh_big` |
| fold / chop / tap / lid | the literal Foley (`chop`, `clink`, `lid`, `tock`), always on the visible action |
| steam | `hiss` bed under macros |
| camel | `camel_bell` (existing in `sfx_extra.py`) |
**Library to have ready** (synthesise locally with `tools/episode_maker/synth.py`; no purchases): whoosh short / long (done), tick, pop, thud, impact, crack, gong, riser, boing, ding, sparkle, bloop, hiss, creak, page, chop, clink (done); **NEW to write**: `sub_drop` (above), `reverse_cymbal` (1.0 s, for the title bloom), `soft_whoosh_low` (2 kHz low-passed, for CALM transitions), `heartbeat_tick` (the existing `heart` at -18 dB for the Dad beat), `room_tone(kind)` (ambience beds with a gentle air layer so "silence" never reaches digital zero).
**Mix.** Voice -16 LUFS short-term target on its own track; music ducked **12-14 dB** under voice (attack 80 ms, release 250 ms) and 6 dB under impacts; SFX peaks <= -3 dBFS, hit SFX no more than voice +4 dB; ambience bed -32 dB (CALM -28 dB). Master: **-14 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1 dBTP**, short-term loudness never more than +6 LU above the voice average (no sudden loud sounds, evidence 18). Do not repeat an identical harsh SFX more than 3 times in 5 s. Test on a phone speaker and with the sound off.
**Silence as a tool.** One breath per episode (section 3): music and ambience fall to -30 dB over 0.1 s, a held sine tone (A or the key's tonic, -30 dB) is allowed; never digital silence. The Dad beat in Episode 2 begins after that breath: the beat has a texture (lantern crackle, soft breeze, a page rustle, a single piano line), not emptiness.
**Ear candy** (small rewards for repeat viewers; each at -12 dB under the voice, one per 8-10 s): the tiny kettle toot after a Narrator line, the camel bell, a stall bell, a different "plink" per Uncle gag, a rhythm of chopsticks that hits the beat grid.
**Premiere only.** The song sting plays 3.5-11 s; the hook's thunder and gong end in silence (0.1 s) before the first sting hit so the song lands as peak 2.

## 7. Flicker rules (photosensitivity) and the automatic check
Rules (evidence 20-22):
1. **At most 3 flashes in any 1 second** (WCAG 2.3.1 / BT.1702); our design budget is **<= 2** (`qc_flash.py --strict`).
2. A "flash" is a pair of opposing luminance changes of >= 10 % of the maximum over >= about 2.8 % of the screen, darker side below 0.80. Hard cuts between very bright and very dark shots count; stripes of the same luminance do not.
3. **Impact frames <= 2 frames** (83 ms at 24 fps); luminance delta <= 0.30 (use cream `#FFFAF0` or a drawn radial burst on a mid-tone, not pure white on black); never two impact frames within 0.5 s; at most 3 in an episode (Episode 1: C01 thunder, K01 title, K02 burn).
4. **No red flashes**: no saturated red (R/(R+G+B) >= 0.8) filling >= 25 % of the picture for under 0.25 s, and no red/blue or red/black alternation; a red silhouette is allowed if it stays under 10 % of the picture and does not alternate.
5. No regular stripe, checker or spiral pattern over more than 25 % of the screen (speed lines are thin, low-contrast, <= 20 % luminance difference, not strobing). (Pattern thresholds in broadcast guidance were not verified in this research: **RE-READ** BT.1702 section on patterns.)
6. Camera shake <= 0.4 s; no continuous oscillation above 3 Hz of the whole frame; no sustained flashing sequence over 5 s of any kind.
7. Glow/bloom and aura effects fade in and out (>= 6 frames), never blink.
**Automatic check:** `python3 tools/episode_maker/qc_flash.py VIDEO [--strict] [--json report.json]` (needs ffmpeg and numpy). It decodes at about 96 px wide, computes linear relative luminance per frame, counts the net signed area of pixels that brighten or darken by >= 0.10 between frames (moving edges cancel), turns frames above the 2.8 % area threshold into transitions, and counts opposing pairs in every 1-s window; it also checks the saturated-red area. PASS = no window above the limit (3, or 2 with `--strict`); a window exactly at the limit is a WARN. Tested 2026-11-28: `episode-01-v4.4.mp4`, `episode-01-v4.4-mobile.mp4`, `episode-01-v4.3-mobile.mp4`, `test43.mp4` all PASS with a maximum of 2.5 flashes/s (the hard cuts of the old edits); synthetic 6 Hz white/black and 4 Hz red test videos FAIL, 3 Hz passes with a WARN, 0.5 Hz passes. It is a screening tool, not a certified Harding FPA: for a premiere, run it on every render (both orientations) and keep the report next to the video; for extra safety a Harding-certified test can be run by a broadcaster later if the series is licensed.

## 8. Design guidance (picture)
- **Big readable shapes**: one clear subject per shot, silhouette readable at thumbnail size on a 6-inch phone; the speaking face >= 25 % of the picture height (already in the shot rules); eye-line at 35-40 % of the frame height (about y 650-770 of 1920).
- **Contrast**: subject against background at least 0.25 difference in relative luminance; backgrounds are painted softer and a step lighter or darker than the subject; the orange sash and the dumpling are the colour accents. Palette accents from `CLAUDE.md`: leaf yellow `#ECC66A` = highlight and active caption word; cream `#FFFAF0` = text, impact wash; orange `#D9480F` = action accent (sash, stress pop, badge); peach `#FFE3B8` = warm light and cards; brown `#3B2A1A` = outlines and shadows. Red `#D9480F`-like oranges are fine as small accents but never as a full-frame wash.
- **Motion salience**: only one thing moves strongly at a time; the background drifts slowly (2-3 px/s) so the foreground action wins; sprite effects (sweat, stars, aura) are the loud layer, the AI clip stays calm; high motion AI clips at most 3 (lint).
- **9:16**: key content in x 60-900, y 270-1440; faces upper-middle; captions below the faces per the Caption column; place and fact cards in the top band; nothing important in the bottom 480 px or the right 180 px column.
- **16:9** (re-framed from the portrait clips where possible): subjects in the centre 60 % width, action-safe 93 %, title-safe 90 % (about 96 px margin), captions in the lower third; punch-ins are scaled from the 16:9 frame, not copied from the portrait crop.
- **Shot variety inside a 3-s clip** comes from the edit (crops at 1.0 / 1.15 / 1.35 scale of the same frame), so one AI clip yields 2-3 distinct shots and the GPU budget stays the same.

## 9. How the scripts carry this (lint conventions, added with this spec)
All in the **EDIT** column (so every script keeps the same table columns). Tokens are separated by `;`, never `|` (a pipe would break the table):
- `PACE fast|medium|calm` (first token): required on every row of a pace-aware script (a script is pace-aware as soon as one row has it).
- `CUTS 0.0:A/0.9:punch/1.6:ins` : picture cuts inside the slot, seconds from the clip start, first is `0.0`, types `A` (the AI clip as generated), `punch` (zoom-punch crop of the same clip), `ins` (insert: detail crop or code sprite), `rx` (reaction crop), `card` (a full card). Spacing >= 0.25 s. Rows without CUTS count as one shot. The lint prints the ASL (overall and per mode) and warns when it leaves the targets of section 0.
- `TRIM 0.3-2.3` : only the window a-b (seconds) of a clip generated at max(b, 2.0) s is used; the slot `Dur` must equal b-a (+/- 0.05 s) and may be 0.8-3.5 s for an AI row. Use it on clips with slack (no line, or a line well under the slot); the model still renders 2.0-3.5 s.
- `MICRO 1.4` (CALM rows longer than 2.5 s): a micro-motion or sound texture at that second; the lint warns on a CALM row over 2.5 s without it.
- `FLASH@0.1 2f cream` / `IMPACT@1.2 2f` : an impact or flash effect at second t of the clip, length in frames, colour. The lint FAILS above 2 frames, FAILS if the colour is red, and FAILS above 2 per absolute second.
`production/v4/shot-rules.md` is updated accordingly (rule 8 and the EDIT convention).

## 10. Checklist per episode (quick)
1. Every row has PACE; FAST/MEDIUM rows have CUTS; CALM rows over 2.5 s have MICRO; lint PASS.
2. Hook shows face + odd thing in frame 0, sound in the first 0.1 s.
3. One fact, said once, in a CALM shot, with a card <= 5 words and a reaction after it.
4. Every cut has a sound; one breath; the Dad beat has texture, not emptiness.
5. After render: `qc_flash.py` PASS on the 9:16 and the 16:9 file; loudness -14 LUFS / <= -1 dBTP; caption bottom <= y 1440.
6. After upload: compare the hook swipe-away and the retention curve with the targets; adjust section 2 numbers, not the story.
