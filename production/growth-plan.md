## 1. The "Made for kids" decision (decide before Episode 1)

This choice changes almost every line of the monetization roadmap, so it comes first.

**Recommendation: write and package for a general/family audience and, if that is honestly true for the video, set "No, not made for kids".** Answer per video; never mark "No" on a video that is truly aimed at young children (YouTube can override the setting, and COPPA penalties apply to creators).

What makes "No" honest for this show (from `series-format.md`): real food history, culture, travel, recipes with grown-up steps, an ongoing story about grief and family, humor that works for adults. Keep it that way:
- Titles and descriptions speak to everyone: "soup dumplings", "Shanghai", "recipe", "history". **Never** "for kids", "for toddlers", "learn colors", nursery-rhyme style.
- Narration in normal conversational English, not toddler-talk.
- Recipe videos aimed at the grown-up who cooks, with kids as helpers ("kid job").
- No toy unboxings, no nursery songs, no "kids, ask your parents" calls to action.

If you choose (or YouTube sets) **"Yes, made for kids"**, the plan still works, but:
- Drop every comment-based tactic (Section 6) on YouTube; move "Where next?" voting to Community posts (also limited on MFK channels, check), the creator page and Instagram/TikTok stories.
- Income shifts to: long recipe videos (contextual ads), Ko-fi, printables/merch sold through the creator page, and **brand deals with family-safe brands**. Memberships and the merch shelf are not available.

Each roadmap step in Section 7 lists the MFK impact.

## 4. Thumbnails and first frames

**Shorts (no custom thumbnail until YPP, see Section 0):**

## 9. KPIs to check weekly (YouTube Studio → Analytics)

| KPI | Where | Healthy early target (indicative, from industry rules of thumb) | If low, do this |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Viewed vs. swiped away** (Shorts) | Content → a Short → Analytics → Engagement | 60%+ viewed | Hook problem. Start on the gag or the steam shot; no title card in the first 3s; add an overlay question in frame 1 |
| **Average percentage viewed** (Shorts) | Same | 70–100%+ (over 100% = rewatches) | Cut slow moments (e.g. long title cards); tighten the "Legend says" beat to 10s; make the ending loop into the start |
| **Subscribers gained per Short** (and per 1,000 views) | Audience tab / per video | Any steady growth; compare episodes | Add a 2-second "Follow Dad's journal" end card; mention "Episode 1 of 3" so viewers know there is more |
| **Returning viewers** | Audience tab | Rising week over week | Stronger cliffhangers ("Next time…"), consistent days, "Start Here" playlist link in every description |
| **Impressions click-through rate** (long videos) | Reach tab | 4–10% | Redo the thumbnail (bigger dumpling, fewer words); try Test & Compare; rewrite the title to the search phrase |
| **Average view duration / retention at 30s** (long videos) | Engagement | Keep 60%+ at 30s | Show the finished dumpling first ("here's what we're making"), skip a long intro |
| **Watch hours (12 months) and subscribers** | Earn tab progress bars | Track monthly vs. the 3,000 h / 8,000 h goals | If Shorts grow but hours don't: add more long-form (compilations, recipes) |
| **Traffic sources** | Reach tab | Shorts feed for Shorts; YouTube search for recipes | If recipe search traffic is low, improve the title/description keywords (Section 5) |
| **Comments per 1,000 views** | Engagement | Any; reply to all | Ask a sharper question in the pinned comment (yes/no or "A or B") |
| **Cross-platform** | TikTok/IG insights | Compare which platform's viewers follow | Double down on the platform with the best follow rate; keep the others as reposts |

**Rules for reading numbers:**
- Judge a Short after 7 days, a long video after 28 days. A new channel's first 5–10 uploads often get very few views; that is normal, not failure.
- Change **one** thing at a time (hook, title or length) and compare with the previous episode.
- Red flag: 4 weeks in a row with <50% viewed vs. swiped away. Then pause new production for one week, rewatch the first 3 seconds of every Short, and re-edit the openings of the next two episodes.
- Never buy views, subs or engagement. It breaks YouTube's rules and poisons the recommendations.
