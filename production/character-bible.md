# Character Bible: The Dumpling Boy

Use the same descriptions **word for word** every time you generate an image, so the
characters look the same in every episode. Save the best result for each character
and reuse it as a reference image ("image-to-image" / "character reference") in later generations.

## Global style (paste at the start of every prompt)
```
2D cartoon, soft rounded shapes, thick dark-brown outlines, warm pastel colors,
cozy food-illustration style, simple clean backgrounds, vertical 9:16 frame
```
Palette: hat-leaf yellow `#ECC66A` · dumpling cream `#FFFAF0` · warm orange `#D9480F` · peach `#FFE3B8` · outline brown `#3B2A1A`

## The Boy ("Bo")
```
A cheerful 10-year-old East Asian boy, round face, big curious dark eyes, short messy black hair,
wearing a traditional Vietnamese conical leaf hat (non la, pointed cone shape like a rice farmer's hat)
tied with a thin orange chin ribbon, a simple white kimono-style wrap robe with an orange cloth belt,
loose white pants, brown sandals, a small canvas shoulder bag with a pair of wooden chopsticks sticking out
```
Personality: calm, curious, poetic. Smiles with his eyes closed when he remembers Dad.

**Signature look:** the pointed conical hat with the orange chin ribbon, plus the white kimono with the orange belt. Keep exactly these in every image.

## The Uncle ("Uncle Fong")
```
A big, round, jolly middle-aged East Asian man, bald head with a thin black mustache,
rosy cheeks, wearing a large simple white kimono-style wrap robe with a wide orange cloth belt around
his big belly, a few small sauce stains, loose white pants and wooden sandals, always holding chopsticks, no hat
```
Personality: loud, warm, impatient, always hungry. Catchphrase before every bite: **"For science!"**

## Dad (memory only)
```
Soft sepia-toned silhouette of a tall slim man from behind, wearing the same Vietnamese conical leaf hat,
standing in warm golden light, gentle dreamy blur, no visible face
```
Never show his face. His voice-over is calm and warm.

## Example full prompt (Episode 1, opening shot)
```
2D cartoon, soft rounded shapes, thick dark-brown outlines, warm pastel colors, cozy food-illustration
style, vertical 9:16 frame. Inside a tiny steamy dumpling shop in Shanghai, a bamboo steamer basket
opening with clouds of steam. Through the steam: a big, round, jolly middle-aged East Asian man,
bald head with a thin black mustache, rosy cheeks, white kimono-style robe with an orange belt,
eyes huge with excitement.
```
