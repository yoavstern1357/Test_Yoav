## CURRENT CANON (v6)
**Look:** refined soft-watercolor anime, our own look (not Ghibli, no studio imitation): gentle clean line art, soft painterly shading, warm muted palette, the orange sash as the family accent. No kimonos, nothing karate-like. Palette: leaf yellow `#ECC66A` · cream `#FFFAF0` · orange `#D9480F` · peach `#FFE3B8` · brown `#3B2A1A`.

**The Boy ("Bo"), age 7. Direction A = POLISH the existing design** (same look, drift removed, locked features): small, round soft face, big dark eyes, short black hair, Vietnamese conical leaf hat with an orange chin ribbon (the ribbon is never missing), faded indigo tunic with side buttons, light hemp trousers rolled to the shin, orange cloth sash, straw sandals (never closed shoes). Dad's journal travels with him. Warm, curious, deadpan humor; eyes-closed smile only for Dad moments.
```
a small 7-year-old boy, 4.75 heads tall, round face, big dark eyes, short black hair, conical leaf hat with an orange chin ribbon, faded indigo tunic with side buttons, light hemp trousers rolled to the shin, orange cloth sash, straw sandals
```

**The Uncle ("Uncle Fong"): variant B (creator, 2026-11-28).** Big, fat, comically ridiculous but sometimes wise; the jokes are on his behavior, never his weight. **Fully bald** round head, thin long black mustache with **curled tips**, thick brows, **earth-brown linen mandarin-collar jacket** (3 knot buttons), **wide orange sash**, **white towel on his left shoulder**, charcoal trousers, straw sandals, never a hat. Catchphrase before every bite: **"For dumpling research!"**
```
the Uncle: a very large, fat, round man, 5.25 heads tall, egg-shaped body with a big round belly, completely bald shiny round head with one small highlight, thick expressive black eyebrows, a thin long black mustache with tips curling upward past his cheeks, small kind dark eyes, round rosy cheeks and a double chin, a loose earth-brown linen work jacket with a mandarin collar and three cloth knot buttons, sleeves pushed to the elbows, a wide orange cloth sash tied in front with two long tails, wide charcoal trousers, straw sandals, a white towel over his left shoulder, long wooden chopsticks tucked in the sash
```

**Grandma, the old dumpling master** (exactly as in `character-redesign-plan.md` 3.3 B): tiny, sturdy, square block; white-grey hair in a tall round bun with two crossed wooden pins; calm half-closed crescent eyes; deep jade-green high-collar jacket, sleeves tied with an orange cord; long cream apron with an orange sash; dark trousers; black cloth shoes; short thin rolling pin in the sash. Her eyes opening is an event.
```
Grandma, the old dumpling master: a tiny, sturdy elderly woman, 4.25 heads tall, square solid body with a low wide stance, white-grey hair pulled back into a tall round bun pierced by two crossed plain wooden pins, calm half-closed crescent eyes with smile lines, small straight brows, a deep jade-green cotton jacket with a high collar, sleeves tied back with an orange cord, a long plain cream apron to mid-shin with an orange apron sash, dark trousers, black cloth shoes, flour on her hands and forearms, a short thin wooden rolling pin tucked in her apron sash
```

**Dad (memory only, unchanged):** a soft sepia silhouette of a tall slim man from behind, wearing the same conical leaf hat, warm golden light, no visible face, ever. Story status: Dad **vanished** and left his dumpling book (never said blunt; one gentle beat at Huế). Ending line of every episode: **"Dad was right..."**

**Recurring cast (see the v6 plan, 3.4-3.5):** the Narrator (epic voice), the Camel (silent), the rival host **"Pepper"** and her producer **"Auntie Clip"** (working names, arrive in Ep 6, design pending), local masters per country ("Dad's Leaf Hat friends"). ## Global style (LEGACY, do not use; paste at the start of every prompt)
The look is made for young kids (soft, round, colorful); the content is for everyone. See `series-format.md`.
```
Soft, gentle children's picture-book animation style, 2D cartoon, very round friendly shapes,
big expressive eyes, soft dark-brown outlines, bright cheerful pastel colors, soft warm lighting,
cozy and safe mood, simple clean backgrounds, vertical 9:16 frame
```
Palette: hat-leaf yellow `#ECC66A` · dumpling cream `#FFFAF0` · warm orange `#D9480F` · peach `#FFE3B8` · outline brown `#3B2A1A`

## The Boy ("Bo")
```
A cheerful 10-year-old East Asian boy, round face, big curious dark eyes, short messy black hair,
wearing a traditional Vietnamese conical leaf hat (non la, pointed cone shape like a rice farmer's hat)
tied with a thin orange chin ribbon, a simple white kimono-style wrap robe with an orange cloth belt,
loose white pants, brown sandals, a small canvas shoulder bag with a pair of wooden chopsticks sticking out
```
Personality: calm, curious, poetic. Smiles with his eyes closed when he remembers Dad.

**Signature look:** the pointed conical hat with the orange chin ribbon, plus the white kimono with the orange belt. Keep exactly these in every image.

## The Uncle ("Uncle Fong")
```
A big, round, jolly middle-aged East Asian man, bald head with a thin black mustache,
rosy cheeks, wearing a large simple white kimono-style wrap robe with a wide orange cloth belt around
his big belly, a few small sauce stains, loose white pants and wooden sandals, always holding chopsticks, no hat
```
Personality: loud, warm, impatient, always hungry. Catchphrase before every bite: **"For science!"**

## Dad (memory only)
```
Soft sepia-toned silhouette of a tall slim man from behind, wearing the same Vietnamese conical leaf hat,
standing in warm golden light, gentle dreamy blur, no visible face
```
Never show his face. His voice-over is calm and warm.
